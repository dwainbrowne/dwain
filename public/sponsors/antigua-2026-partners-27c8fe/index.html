Practical skills for everyday business work

The target audience includes trades and construction businesses, heavy-duty equipment businesses, service industries and property management firms. The audience mix, attendance and final capacity are still to be confirmed.

The evening uses worked examples and time to map one recurring business task. Attendees explore ways software and AI can help, with people reviewing outputs before use. Any product or consulting next step remains optional.

Job to invoice

Mapping the steps between a customer request and money received, and finding where time and details get lost.

AI drafts, checked by people

Using AI to draft quotes, emails and summaries, with a person reviewing every one before it goes out.

Paperwork in order

Organizing sample receipts and invoices, naming files clearly and flagging missing information for review.

What each attendee takes home

  • A workflow map for one recurring business task
  • A prompt kit for everyday business drafts
  • A checklist for reviewing AI output before it is used
  • A seven-day plan for putting one change into practice

A clear, approved introduction

Each confirmed sponsor supplies one approved, factual one-line description and an invitation to speak to an approved named contact about an approved topic. Attendees choose whether to get in touch.

The organizer’s event reminder and recap emails will include a proposed “Sponsored by” section with approved logos, descriptions and contact links. The Lead Sponsor receives prominent placement; Supporters appear together. The number and timing of emails will be agreed in writing.

Logo use, introductions and contact links require approval before use. No attendee list, identities or contact details are shared with sponsors.

What sponsorship does and does not include

Protected

  • The educational content is set by the organizer and is not shaped by sponsors.
  • The organizer keeps control of the agenda, materials, slides and acknowledgements.
  • Benefits, payment and cancellation terms are agreed in writing before any commitment.
  • Acknowledgement wording and logo use are approved before they appear.

Not included

  • No speaking slot or product presentation
  • No category or event exclusivity
  • No endorsement of a sponsor's products or services
  • No attendee contact details or lists
  • No recording or replay rights
  • No guaranteed attendance, reach, leads or sales
