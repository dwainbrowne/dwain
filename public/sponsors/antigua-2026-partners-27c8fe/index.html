Practical skills for everyday business work

The target audience includes trades and construction businesses, heavy-duty equipment businesses, service industries and property management firms. The audience mix, attendance and final capacity are still to be confirmed.

The evening uses worked examples and time to map one recurring business task. Attendees explore ways software and AI can help, with people reviewing outputs before use. Any product or consulting next step remains optional.

Job to invoice

Mapping the steps between a customer request and money received, and finding where time and details get lost.

AI drafts, checked by people

Using AI to draft quotes, emails and summaries, with a person reviewing every one before it goes out.

Paperwork in order

Organizing sample receipts and invoices, naming files clearly and flagging missing information for review.

What each attendee takes home

  • A workflow map for one recurring business task
  • A prompt kit for everyday business drafts
  • A checklist for reviewing AI output before it is used
  • A seven-day plan for putting one change into practice
