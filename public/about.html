Dwain Browne

Dwain Browne is a technology entrepreneur, software developer, and cloud infrastructure architect with experience building software platforms, automation systems, and AI-driven business tools.

He is the Founder and CEO of SnapSuite and LeadScore AI, where he focuses on helping businesses improve operations, sales execution, workflow automation, and decision-making through modern software and artificial intelligence.

Dwain has spent more than two decades working across software systems, SaaS products, logistics technology, business automation, and cloud-based systems. His work is shaped by both founder-level business context and hands-on software execution.

In addition to his technical background, Dwain has practical experience working with businesses in field services, logistics, sales operations, and automation. His perspective as both a founder and builder allows him to understand how technology, infrastructure, and execution can support long-term business growth.

The backstory

Before the companies and platforms, my career started with design. I wanted to understand how things looked, how they communicated, and how people experienced them.

From there, I moved into website development because I wanted to build the things I was designing. That opened the door to software, business workflows, customer problems, and eventually entrepreneurship.

After starting my own website design company, I discovered the skilled trades market and saw how much operational work was still happening through paper, phone calls, disconnected tools, and manual follow-up. That became the foundation for SnapSuite.

As I spent more time around business owners and sales teams, I kept seeing another problem: cold outreach and lead prioritization were taking too much manual effort. That led to LeadScore AI.
