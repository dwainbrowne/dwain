Affordly app

The simplest financial planner is the one people will actually use.

Affordly is positioned around a small, practical promise: track expenses, see your P&L, and build better habits without needing a heavy finance system.

The product keeps attention on the decisions that matter each month: what came in, what went out, what is uncategorized, and where spending is drifting.
