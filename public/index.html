SnapSuite

Running a trade business in the age of AI.

SnapSuite is software for contractors and skilled trades teams that want to get paid faster. It helps contractors, HVAC, plumbers, electrical companies, and garage door companies manage the work that happens between first call and final payment.

It is shaped around the questions owners ask every day: what is incomplete, who is dispatched, which jobs are ready, and how much work is still waiting to be invoiced.

LeadScore AI

Know which leads deserve your next call.

LeadScore AI started inside SnapSuite. Selling software to contractors and service businesses, the team needed a better way to decide who to call next, so they built a scoring model and pointed their own reps at it.

It ranks leads against your ideal customer profile and gives every score a written explanation, so sales teams, B2B sellers, and manufacturers know why a lead is at the top before they pick up the phone.

Explore LeadScore AI Visit getleadscore.ai

What guides it

  • A score needs a reason a rep can read out loud on a call
  • Human beats bot: AI carries the repetitive work, never the conversation
  • Recency is truth: scores decay and timing matters
  • Compliance is a hard gate: do-not-contact means exactly that
